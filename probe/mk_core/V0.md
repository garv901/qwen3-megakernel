## Fix before any data: v0 crashed at every point (job 9568, 9569)
`build.py:dyn_smem` counted 16 attention positions, the placeholder uses SKV_CTX = 128 (12416 vs 41088 B): at NPAD 16..64 the
mbarriers and the TMEM slot lay past the dynamic smem allocation. Fixed in 86a16ba, with a compile-time static_assert
(`MK_EXPECT_DYN == DYN_SMEM`) and a device trap on `%dynamic_smem_size < DYN_SMEM`.
